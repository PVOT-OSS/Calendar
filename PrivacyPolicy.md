# Calendar Privacy Policy
Calendar is an open source application that respects your privacy. The Calendar app does not send any of your data to us. Also Calendar does not use the Internet permission at all.

### What personal information do we collect from the people that visit our blog, website or app?

Calendar does not collect information from the users.

### When do we collect information?

Calendar does not collect information from the users.

### How do we use your information?

We do not send your information or use it at all.

### Do we use 'cookies'?

We do not use cookies for tracking purposes.

### What does the app read on my device?

Everything below stays on your device and is only used to show your calendar:

- **Calendars** (Android calendar storage): to show, create and change your events.
- **Contacts**, only if you turn on *Birthdays* in Settings → General → Extra calendars: birthdays
  and anniversaries saved in your contacts are copied into a local "Birthdays" calendar. Contacts
  are also read to suggest guests while you type.
- **Tasks**, only if you turn on *Tasks*: dated tasks from OpenTasks or Tasks.org are shown in the
  calendar, and ticking one off changes it in that app.
- **Holidays** come from data bundled with the app; nothing is downloaded.
- **Imported .ics files** are read when you open them with the app.

The app has no Internet permission, so none of this can leave your device through it.
